# Student-life editorial sources

Reviewed on 2026-10-04. The Persian and English `life` summaries in `frontend/lib/destination-content.ts` are original paraphrases, not quotations. They describe social life, everyday routines and accommodation without promising identical experiences or services at every institution. No price, immigration, employment-rights or eligibility guidance has been added.

| Destination | Sources | Content supported |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canada | [EduCanada — Housing and accommodations](https://www.educanada.ca/study-plan-etudes/before-avant/housing-logement.aspx?lang=eng) | Residence friendships and campus access; room/kitchen/meal options; off-campus responsibilities; student-association housing guidance. |
| Germany | [Study in Germany / DAAD — Finding accommodation](https://www.study-in-germany.com/en/plan-your-studies/preparation/accomodation/); [Study in Germany — Balancing study and leisure](https://www.study-in-germany.com/en/community/how-to-balance-study-and-leisure-in-germany/) | Dormitories and WGs, social/language benefits, early housing search and International Office support. Leisure suggestions draw on a student ambassador's experience and are presented as options, not universal facts. |
| United Kingdom | [British Council — Student life](https://study-uk.britishcouncil.org/why-study/student-life); [British Council — Support while you study](https://study-uk.britishcouncil.org/moving-uk/support-while-study) | Societies, cultural activities, museums and student discounts; unions and campus support. |
| Italy | [Universitaly — Living and studying in Italy](https://www.universitaly.it/it); [Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs — Useful information for your stay](https://studyinitaly.esteri.it/Static/InformazioniSoggiorno) | Art/history/culture and university accommodation offices and allocation conditions. The summary does not generalise scholarship-specific rules. |
| Netherlands | [Nuffic / Study in NL — Student life](https://www.studyinnl.org/life-in-nl/student-life); [Study in NL — Information package](https://www.studyinnl.org/information-package); [Study in NL — EEA information package](https://www.studyinnl.org/information-package-eea) | Off-campus student culture, associations/ESN, leisure, housing shortage and bicycle/rail travel. Only general transport information is used from the EEA page. |
| Australia | [Study Australia — Connect with your student community](https://www.studyaustralia.gov.au/id/tools-and-resources/tips-and-advice-for-students/how-to-connect-with-your-student-community); [Study Australia — On-campus and local support](https://www.studyaustralia.gov.au/en/life-in-australia/student-support-services/on-campus-and-local-support.html) | Orientation, clubs, social events and provider-specific support. The first page was served in English despite its `/id/` URL. |
| Sweden | [Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences — Living in Sweden](https://www.slu.se/en/study/student-life/the-swedish-way/living-in-sweden/) | Fika, nature/cultural traditions, English communication and regional climate differences. |
| Finland | [Finnish National Agency for Education / Study in Finland — Life in Finland](https://www.studyinfinland.fi/life-in-finland); [About Study in Finland](https://www.studyinfinland.fi/about-study-in-finland) | Informal collaborative learning, independence, flexible study, personal-life balance and nature. Scheduling advice is editorial guidance, not a promised outcome. |
| Denmark | [Study in Denmark — Student life](https://studyindenmark.dk/study-in-denmark/student-life); [International Students' Survival Guide, student-life chapter](https://studyindenmark.dk/why-denmark/what-to-expect/SURVIVALGUIDE2.0.pdf) | Student houses, cultural/nature activities, shared housing, independent daily routines and commuting. |
| New Zealand | [Education New Zealand / NauMai NZ — Life on campus](https://www.naumainz.studywithnewzealand.govt.nz/studying-in-nz/study-experience/life-on-campus) | O Week, campus services, clubs, Māori culture, buddy programmes and volunteering. |
