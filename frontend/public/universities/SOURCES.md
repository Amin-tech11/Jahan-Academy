# University card sources

The campus images in this folder are resized copies of public-domain/CC0 photos on Wikimedia Commons:

- `dalhousie.jpg` — [Dalhousie U 1.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dalhousie_U_1.jpg), Robert Alfers, public domain.
- `laval.jpg` — [Universite Laval building in snow.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Universite_Laval_building_in_snow.jpg), Wilfredor, CC0 1.0.
- `mcgill.jpg` — [Arts Building, McGill University, Aug 31 2022.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Arts_Building,_McGill_University,_Aug_31_2022.jpg), D. Benjamin Miller, CC0 1.0.

Card summaries and locations are based on [Dalhousie](https://www.dal.ca/about.html), [Université Laval](https://www.ulaval.ca/en/about-us/universite-laval-at-a-glance), and [McGill](https://www.mcgill.ca/about/) official university pages.

`dalhousie-logo.jpg` was supplied by the user for use on the Dalhousie card.
