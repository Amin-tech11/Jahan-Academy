# Destination flag sources

These are circular SVG flag icons from [HatScripts/circle-flags](https://github.com/HatScripts/circle-flags), distributed under the MIT license. Their square, circular artwork matches the round flag badges in the reference and is used in home destination cards, university country filters, and the shared navigation menu.

- `australia.svg` — ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 `au`, from [Circle Flags](https://github.com/HatScripts/circle-flags/blob/gh-pages/flags/au.svg).
