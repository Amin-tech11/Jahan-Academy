# Destination cost and planning sources

Reviewed 2026-10-04. The bilingual `planning` fields in `frontend/lib/destination-content.ts` are original summaries. Each contains an introduction and four practical topics: tuition, living expenses, initial costs, and funding/budget management. Local currencies and monthly/annual periods are stated wherever numbers appear. Figures are guide estimates, not quotes or immigration maintenance thresholds; the page asks readers to confirm current costs with their institution and housing provider. No exchange-rate conversions or work-entitlement promises are made.
