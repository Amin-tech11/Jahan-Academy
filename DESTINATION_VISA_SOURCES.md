# Destination admission and visa sources

Reviewed: 2026-10-04. Scope: introductory admission and student visa/residence guidance for the ten destination pages, in Persian and English. Copy is original paraphrase. Programme, nationality and personal circumstances determine the applicable route; the UI directs applicants to confirm the current official checklist. These summaries do not replace complete requirements or promise admission or a visa. No fees, processing-time promises, work rights or post-study eligibility are asserted.

| Country | Official sources | Facts used |

## Content decisions

- Residence permits are named separately where that is the country's route; the section label remains admission and visas for navigation.
- Canada's permission is described as a study permit rather than an entry visa.
- PAL/TAL, ATAS, medical tests and other conditional requirements are not presented as universal.
- Dutch applicants are directed to the institution-led process.
- Both language variants retain the same scope and qualifications.
- Existing local destination photos are reused; this change introduces no runtime external requests.
